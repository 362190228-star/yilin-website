# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This is Yilin Cai's personal portfolio website for interviewers, design leads, recruiters, and hiring teams evaluating her fit for UX, interaction design, and AI product design roles. Visitors usually arrive with limited time and need to understand her professional direction, practical project experience, and design judgment quickly.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Yilin as a professional, clear, and spirited AI Native UX designer. It should make her background, internships, AI product work, project case studies, and contact paths easy to scan while leaving a memorable personal impression beyond a standard resume page. Success means a hiring viewer can quickly see credible experience, specific design strengths, and reasons to continue into her projects or reach out.

## Brand Personality

Professional, fresh, and spirited. The tone should feel confident and approachable: serious enough for design leadership review, light enough to carry personal energy, and specific enough to avoid generic portfolio language.

## Anti-references

This should not feel like an ordinary resume page, a template-heavy student portfolio, a corporate SaaS landing page, or a decorative personal homepage where style hides the work. Avoid generic grids of identical cards, repeated section-label scaffolding, vague self-introduction copy, and overdone visual effects that distract from project substance.

## Design Principles

- Lead with credible design signal before decoration.
- Make the career story scannable for busy hiring viewers.
- Let personal energy appear through pacing, motion, imagery, and details rather than gimmicks.
- Turn AI product experience into a clear positioning thread across internships and projects.
- Keep every section useful: each fold should answer why Yilin is worth interviewing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special accessibility requirements were specified. Default to clear reading contrast, mobile-friendly layouts, keyboard-accessible interactions, descriptive alt text, and reduced-motion support for animated elements.
